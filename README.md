# Andes Capital Real Estate Analysis

Interactive real estate business analysis built in **Tableau**, focused on executive performance, commercial drivers, customer segments, and cohort behavior.

## Live Dashboard

[View the interactive Tableau Public story](https://public.tableau.com/views/AndesCapitalRealEstateDashboard/AndesCapitalRealEstateAnalysis?:language=es-ES&:sid=&:redirect=auth&:display_count=n&:origin=viz_share_link)

## Project Overview

This project analyzes Andes Capital's real estate sales performance across **2023–2024**. The goal is to transform transactional data into an executive view that supports decisions on revenue, property mix, sales channels, customer segments, and customer recurrence.

The Tableau story is organized into three views:

1. **Executive Overview** — KPIs, revenue trend, city performance, and YoY growth.
2. **Commercial Analysis** — property types, sales channels, customer segments, and a conditional-formatting performance table.
3. **Customer Cohort Analysis** — acquisition cohorts and repeat-purchase behavior over time.

## Key Findings

- **Houses** generated the highest total revenue, while **Commercial properties** had the highest average ticket and **Apartments** led sales volume.
- **Mexico City** generated more revenue than Bogotá.
- The **Broker** channel accounted for approximately **73% of total revenue**.
- **First-Time Buyers** represented approximately **63% of total revenue**.
- Revenue showed recurring peaks around **March–April** and **September–November**.
- Initial cohort sales declined from **224 in Jan 2023** to **26 in Dec 2024**.
- Repeat purchases remained present across cohorts, with periodic rebounds during the same seasonal windows.
